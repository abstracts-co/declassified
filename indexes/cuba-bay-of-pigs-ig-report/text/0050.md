reliance would have to be placed on this method of supply for
guerrilla forces.

forces of army and militia in both Las Villas and Oriente

6. In the Sierra Escambray, which is the only area of
Cuba in which true guerrilla operations are now being conducted,
ill-equipped and untrained groups of up to 200 to 300 men have
been hard pressed to survive and have been unable to conduct
effective operations. The anly worthwhile accomplishment of
these bands has been to serve as a symbol of resistance.
Smaller groups, even though better trained and equipped, could
not be expected to be effective. _
7. ‘There are very few sites on the south coast of the
Sierra Escambray where small boats can be landed. These are
found principally at the mouths of rivers and are all guarded
by militia posts armed with machine guns. A small group
landing at such a point by shuttling from a larger vessel in
small boats would probably receive heavy casualties.
8. Small-scale infiltrations would not produce a
psychological effect sufficient to precipitate general uprisings
