RESULTS s.
(S-NFD) The rendezvous was effected and photos were taken of the

could do. Probably the test aircraft would be unable to track it using
the same maneuver.
(S-NFD) The test aircraft then set up and fired the 30mm cannon.
Ten rounds were expended in a 1/2 second buret indicating a rate of
about 1200 rounds a minute. The rounds were fired at a slant range
of atout 10,000'; none of the observers saw the impacts. The muzzle
flashes were very large and bright.
