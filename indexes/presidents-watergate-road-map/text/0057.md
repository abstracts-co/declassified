# 52. Statement on informing the prosecutors

## Page 61

52. On August 22, 1973, the President said at a news conference,
in response to the question why he did not immediately turn
over information concerning criminal wrong-doing to the
prosecutors in March and April 1973, that he assumed that
John Dean in March was’ telling Henry Petersen everything
that Dean was telling the President; the President implied
-chat-he assumed the same about John Ehrlichman after March
30, 1973.
