# 22. The President meets Dean on the morning of April 16

## Page 28

22. On the morning of April 16, 1973, the President met with
John Dean during which meeting there was discussion of
(a) what the President would say publicly the next day
about Watergate, (b) what Dean would testify to concerning
any discussions Dean had had with the President (including
Dean's discussion with the President on March 21 about
Hunt's demand), and (c) the payment to Hunt.
