# 27. Haldeman listens to the March 21 tape

## Page 33

27. On or about April 25 and April 26, 1973, H. R. Haldeman,
at the President's instructions, listened to one or more
tape recordings of Presidential conversations including
the recording of a meeting on the morning of March 21, 1973,
made notes thereon, and reported to the President.

27.1 Grand Jury Testimony of H.R. Haldeman, January 30,
1974, pp. 25-31.
.27.2 H.R. Haldeman's notes of listening to Item 5.1.
