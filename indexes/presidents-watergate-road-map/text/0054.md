# 49. Statement on pressing to get the story out

## Page 58

On August 22, 1973, the President said at a press conference
that at a meeting on March 22, 1973, with Messrs. Mitchell,
Haldeman, Ehrlichman, and Dean, "I kept pressing. for the view
that I had had throughout, that we must get this story out,
get the truth out, whatever and whoever it's going to hurt."
