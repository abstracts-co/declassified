# 13. Ehrlichman tells Krogh to hang tough

## Page 18

13. On or about the afternoon of March 22, 1973, John
Ehrlichman had a conversation with Egil Krogh in which
Ehrlichman stated that Krogh should "hang tough" and
that Hunt was "more stable" and would not “disclose all.”

13.1 Grand Jury Testimony of Egil Krogh, January 29,
1974, pp. 10-11.
