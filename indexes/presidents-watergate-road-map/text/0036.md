# 32. Missing Mitchell call of June 20, 1972

## Page 39

32. On or about dune 20, 1972, the President had a telephone
conversation with John Mitchell. The Presidential tape
recording of this telephone conversation was subpoenaed by
the Grand Jury but was not produced, with the explanation
that the telephone instrument on which the conversation
took place was not connected to the White House taping
system.
