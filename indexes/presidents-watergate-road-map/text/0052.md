# 47. Statements on getting out the facts and Dean's investigation

## Page 55

On August 15, 1973, the President said in a public state-
ment that "My consistent position from the beginning has_
been to get out the facts about Watergate, not to cover
them up." On August 22, 1973, the President said that in
June 1972 he had talked with Clark MacGregor, who had assured
“the President that MacGregor would conduct “a thorough investi”
gation" of possible involvement by the Committee to Re-Elect
the President in the Watergate break-in. The President also
Said that responsibility to investigate possible White House
involvement was given to Johs Ehrlichman, who, in turn, *
delegated the responsibility to John Dean. On August 29,
1972, the President said in a press conference that on the
-basis of "a complete investigation" by John Dean "I can say
categorically that ... . no one ifi the White Houst staff, no
one in this Administration, presently employed, was involved
in this very bizarre incident." On March 2, 1973, the Presi-
dent publicly reiterated this conclusion.
