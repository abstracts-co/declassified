# 7. Mitchell authorizes LaRue to pay Hunt

## Page 12

In or about the early afternoon of March 21, 1973,
John Mitchell had a telephone conversation with Fred
LaRue during which Mitchell authorized LaRue to pay
approximately $75,000 to E. Howard Hunt.

7.1 Grand Jury Testimony of Fred LaRue, February 13,
1974, pp. 7-10A.
