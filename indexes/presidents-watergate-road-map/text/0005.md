# 3. Dean tells Ehrlichman of the message

## Page 7

On or about March 19, 1973, John Dean had a conversation
with John Ehrlichman in which Dean told Ehrlichman of
Hunt's message and in which Ehrlichman asked Dean to
relay the message to John Mitchell.
