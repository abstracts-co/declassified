# 38. Haldeman approves the $350,000 fund transfer

## Page 45

38. In or about early December 1972 and early January 1973,
H. R. Haldeman approved transfer of money from a secret
White House cash fund of $350,000 to be used for payments
to and for the benefit of the Watergate defendants.

38.1 Grand Jury Testimony of John Dean, November 20,
1973, pp. 34-36.
38.2 Grand Jury Testimony of H. R. Haldeman, January
30, 1974, pp. 124-126.
