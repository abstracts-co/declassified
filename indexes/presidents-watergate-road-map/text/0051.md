# 46. Statements on knowledge of a cover-up

## Page 54

46. On August 15, 1973, the President said in a public statement
that "Not: only was I unaware of any cover~up, but...
until March 21st, I was unaware that there was anything to
cover-up." On August 22, 1973, the President said ina
press conference that "when we met on Sept. 15 and again
throughout our discussions in the month of March, Mr. Dean
insisted there was not -- and I used [sic] his words -- a
‘scintilla of evidence’ indicating that anyone on the White
House staff was involved in the planning of, the Watergate
break-in." On August 15, 1973, the President said in a public
statement that on March 21, 1973, "I was told .. . for
the first time that the planning of the Watergate went
beyond those who had been tried and convicted, and that at
; least one, and possibly more, persons at the Re-Election
Committee were involved."
