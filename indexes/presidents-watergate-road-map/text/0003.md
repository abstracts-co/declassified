On or about March 16, 1973, E. Howard Hunt had a meeting
with Paul O'Brien during which Hunt demanded approximately
$120,000 and asked O'Brien to tell John Dean that Hunt had
done some "“seamy things” for the White House and for John
Ehrlichman and that, if Hunt were not paid soon, Hunt would
have to “review his options."
