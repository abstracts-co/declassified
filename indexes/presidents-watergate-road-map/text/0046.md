# 42. March 13, 1973 meeting with Dean

## Page 49

42. On March 13, 1973, the President had a meeting with
John Dean during which there was discussion of the pre-
June 17, 1972, role of Gordon Strachan in Watergate and
Strachan's statements to investigators; the possible
public testimony of Maurice Stans, Herbert Kalmbach, and
Hugh Sloan; the pre-dJune 17, 1972, role of Jeb Magruder in
Watergate; the status of H. R. Haldeman, the role of Mitchell
and Haldeman regarding Liddy's intelligence program at CRP;
and the inadvisability of making full disclosure.

42.1 Tape recording of a meeting between the President
and John Dean on March 13, 1973, and transcript
thereof.
