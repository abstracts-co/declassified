# 35. Gray warns the President

## Page 42

On July 6, 1972, the President had a telephone conversation
with L. Patrick Gray during which Gray warned the President
that the President's too aides were "mortally wounding" him
and/or were not cooperating with the FBI's investigation,
and/or that "the matter of Watergate might lead higher."
