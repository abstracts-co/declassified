# 39. Dean doubts Executive Privilege would hold

## Page 46

|
39. In or about late February, John Dean had a conversation
with John Ehrlichman during which Dean stated that a
claim of Executive Privilege with respect to Dean's
possible testimony about Watergate might not be sustained
because Dean had had so few meetings with the President.

39.1 Grand Jury Testimony of John Dean, February 14,
1974, pp. 7-8.
