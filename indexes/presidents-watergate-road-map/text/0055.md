# 50. Statement on Ehrlichman's inquiry

## Page 59

50. On August 15, 1973, the President said in a public statement
that "When I learned on March 30th that Mr. Dean had been
unable to complete his report, I instructed Mr. Ehrlichman
to conduct an independent inquiry and bring all the facts to
me. . On April 14, Mr.-Ehrlichman gave me his findings, and
I directed that he report them to the Attorney General
immediately.”

1973, pp. 138-140.
50.2 Grand Jury Testimony of Richard Kleindienst, August 9,
1973, pp. 67-69.
