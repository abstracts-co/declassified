# 12. The President proposes a Dean report

## Page 17

12. On the afternoon of March 22, 1973, the President met
with John Mitchell, H. R. Haldeman, John Ehrilichman,
and John Dean during which meeting there was discussion
of a possible course of action that might be taken with
respect to the matters discussed on the morning of
March 21, 1973, namely, preparation by Dean of a written
“report” -- stating that high White House officials were
not involved in the Watergate break-in ~~ on which the
President could at a later time “rely,” if necessary.
