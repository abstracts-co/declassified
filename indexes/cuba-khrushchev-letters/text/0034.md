They say that so far as it is a matter of formalizing the

I heard that Americans have a rule: in any business each
side should approach with the same standard the fulfilment of
both its own obligations and the obligations of its counter­
part and not use "double standard" -one for itself and another
for the others. This is a good rule and if it is observed this
promises a prompt settlement of the Cuban conflict. Let us
follow this good American rule.
