Re: Norman Mailer
A review in Time Magazine on 7/16/73, a Mike Wallace
television interview, a 7/22/73 New York Times book review by Pauline
Kael, and a Washington Post article dated 7/20/78 by Stephen Isaacs,
are all critical of Mailer for his unsubstantiated theorizing and for
engaging in "yellow" journalism.
ACTION:
For information only. No action is recommended regarding
Mailer's allegations. Any public statements by the FBI would merely
serve to feed the fires of publicity which Matler is attempting to stoke.
