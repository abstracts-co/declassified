In other chapters we have discussed the formation of a plasma cloud
due to x-irradiation of the lunar surface by the detonation of a nuclear device.
Calculations were made on the energy deposition in various layers of the
lunar surface for typical weapon temperatures. The resulting rock vapor will,
of course, rarefy out into space from the lunar surface. Since it will have
