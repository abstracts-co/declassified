Now what is the possibility that microorganisms deposited on the moon
will actually be shielded? The nature of the lunar surface is a complex and
14
much debated problem, which need riot be reviewed here. But it is of in-
terest to call attention to a few points. From eclipse temperature measurements
and radio observations it is known that there is a dust covering on the moon,
but estimates of its depth range from millimeters to miles. However, Whlpple15
has called attention to the experimental fact that dust, in a vacuum, and ir-

such positions as to be shielded from the sun's rays at all angles of insola-

dormant anaerobic microorganisms deposited near the surface would seem
to be a possibility which cannot be neglected. A determination of the micro-
structue of the moon's surface material is therefore of great importance.
