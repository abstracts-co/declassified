was to put things back together and close up the crate. The ﬁrst
job, re-securing the orb in its basket, proved to be the most ticklish
and time-consuming part of the whole nights work. The baﬂle plate

theloutlet on beyond the baﬁle plate. We spent almost an hour on

precisely the right position and one in the engine compartment trying
to engage the threads on the end of a rod he couldn't see. Aftera
number of futile attempts and many anxious moments, the connection
was ﬁnally made, and we all sighed with relief. ,
