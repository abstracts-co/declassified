## UFOs

**Hottel Memo** (Document, FBI, 1950, 2 pages)

A one-page memo of 22 March 1950 from Guy Hottel, head of the FBI's Washington field office, to J. Edgar Hoover, passing on a third party's story that an Air Force investigator said three "flying saucers" with three-foot-tall occupants had been recovered in New Mexico. It is the most viewed file in the FBI Vault; the FBI says it never investigated the claim and sees no reason to link it to Roswell.

Released 1977. [Official source](https://vault.fbi.gov/hottel_guy) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=ufo-hottel-memo)

**Gemini 7 Bogey Audio** (Recording, NASA, 1965, 6:12)

NASA air-to-ground and public-affairs audio from the December 1965 Gemini VII flight, in which Frank Borman reports an unidentified object he calls a "bogey" to mission control in Houston. The Pentagon published it in its first PURSUE release in May 2026; space historian James Oberg attributes it to debris from the mission's own booster.

Released 2026-05-08. [Official source](https://www.dvidshub.net/video/1006119/nasa-audio-12-5-1965-low-earth-orbit) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=ufo-gemini-7-bogey)

**Apollo 17 Three Dots** (Photo, NASA, 1972)

A NASA photograph from the Apollo 17 mission in December 1972, published in the Pentagon's first PURSUE release with an enlarged inset of three faint dots in a triangle in the lunar sky. The Pentagon said there is no consensus on what they are; critics note the Apollo photos had been public for decades.

Released 2026-05-08. [Official source](https://www.war.gov/UFO/) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=ufo-apollo-17-dots)

**The Roswell Report: Case Closed** (Document, US Air Force, 1997, 232 pages)

The US Air Force's 1997 report on the Roswell legend. It concludes that the "alien bodies" witnesses described were anthropomorphic test dummies carried aloft by high-altitude balloons, and that stories of bodies at the base hospital blended a 1956 KC-97 crash and a 1959 balloon accident.

Released 1997-06-24. [Official source](https://www.af.mil/The-Roswell-Report/) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=ufo-roswell-case-closed)

**FLIR1 (Tic Tac)** (Film, US Navy, 2004, 1:16)

Infrared targeting-pod video from a US Navy F/A-18 of the USS Nimitz strike group, recorded off Southern California on 14 November 2004 during the encounter pilots described as a white "Tic Tac". It leaked in 2007, the Pentagon released it officially in April 2020, and AARO lists it as unresolved.

Released 2020-04-27. [Official source](https://www.dvidshub.net/video/955825/flir-uap) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=ufo-flir1-tic-tac)

**Gimbal** (Film, US Navy, 2015, 0:34)

Infrared video from a US Navy F/A-18 of the USS Theodore Roosevelt strike group off the US East Coast in January 2015, in which the aircrew track an object that appears to rotate. It leaked in 2017, the Pentagon released it officially in April 2020, and AARO lists it as unresolved.

Released 2020-04-27. [Official source](https://www.dvidshub.net/video/956955/gimbal-uap) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=ufo-gimbal)

**Go Fast** (Film, US Navy, 2015, 0:34)

Infrared video from a US Navy F/A-18F off the coast of Florida in January 2015 that seems to show a small object racing low over the ocean. The Pentagon released it officially in April 2020; AARO's February 2025 case resolution put the object at about 13,000 feet and found with high confidence that it did not move at anomalous speeds.

Released 2020-04-27. [Official source](https://www.dvidshub.net/video/956683/gofast-uap) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=ufo-gofast)

**ODNI Preliminary Assessment on UAP** (Document, ODNI, 2021, 9 pages)

The Director of National Intelligence's unclassified report to Congress on UAP, drafted with the Pentagon's UAP Task Force. Of 144 US government reports from 2004 to 2021 it could explain only one, a large deflating balloon, and it noted 18 incidents with unusual movement patterns or flight characteristics.

Released 2021-06-25. [Official source](https://www.dni.gov/index.php/newsroom/reports-publications/reports-publications-2021/item/2223-preliminary-assessment-unidentified-aerial-phenomena) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=ufo-odni-preliminary-assessment)

## JFK

**Schlesinger CIA Reorganization Memo** (Document, White House, 1961, 16 pages)

Kennedy aide Arthur Schlesinger Jr.'s memo after the Bay of Pigs proposing to split the CIA into an operations agency and a research agency, with clandestine work cleared by the State Department. The March 2025 release removed the last redactions, which show the CIA had nearly as many people under official cover abroad as the State Department.

Released 2025-03-18. [Official source](https://www.archives.gov/research/jfk/release-2025) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=jfk-schlesinger-cia-memo)

**Mexico City Mystery Man** (Photo, CIA, 1963)

A CIA photo of an unidentified man taken in Mexico City in October 1963, which CIA staff at first wrongly linked to Oswald; it became Warren Commission Exhibit 237. The FBI showed it to Oswald's mother on 23 November 1963, and neither the CIA, the FBI nor the House Assassinations Committee could identify the man, who was neither Oswald nor Ruby.

Released 1964-11-23. [Official source](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/348806259) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=jfk-mystery-man)

**CE 399, the "Magic Bullet"** (Photo, Warren Commission, 1963)

A National Archives photograph of Commission Exhibit 399, the nearly whole bullet found on a stretcher at Parkland Memorial Hospital. The Warren Commission said it came from the rifle found in the Book Depository and that one bullet likely hit both Kennedy and Governor Connally; critics of that single-bullet theory call it the "magic bullet".

Released 1964-11-23. [Official source](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/305144) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=jfk-ce-399-bullet)

**LBJ Sworn In Aboard Air Force One** (Photo, White House, 1963)

Released 1963-11-22. [Official source](https://www.lbjlibrary.org/object/photo/swearing-lyndon-b-johnson-president) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=jfk-lbj-oath-photo)

## Area 51

**U-2 in Fake NASA Markings** (Photo, NASA, 1960)

After Francis Gary Powers' CIA U-2 was shot down over the Soviet Union on 1 May 1960, NASA said the plane had been doing weather research. To back the cover story, a U-2 was painted in NASA markings with a made-up serial number and shown to reporters at NASA's Flight Research Center at Edwards on 6 May 1960.

Released 1960-05-06. [Official source](https://www.dvidshub.net/image/687121/u-2-with-fictitious-nasa-markings-support-cia-cover-story-pilot-gary-powers-shot-down-over-s) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=area51-u2-nasa-cover)

**A-12 OXCART in Flight** (Photo, US Air Force, 1965)

An undated 1960s U.S. Air Force photo of the A-12 OXCART, the Mach 3 spy plane Lockheed built for the CIA and test-flew at Area 51 from April 1962. This aircraft, 60-6932, was lost at sea with CIA pilot Jack Weeks in June 1968, shortly before the program ended.

Released 1982. [Official source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A12-flying.jpg) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=area51-a12-oxcart)

**M-21 Carrying a D-21 Drone** (Photo, US Air Force, 1965)

An undated U.S. Air Force photo from the 1964-66 test program of M-21 60-6940, a two-seat A-12 rebuilt as a mothership, carrying a D-21 reconnaissance drone on its back. On 30 July 1966 a D-21 struck the other M-21 as it launched, destroying both and killing launch officer Ray Torick; the drone was later launched from B-52s over China instead.

Released 1982. [Official source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lockheed_M-21_with_D-21_drone_in_flight_c1965.JPEG) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=area51-d21-drone)

**Project HAVE DOUGHNUT** (Document, DIA, 1969, 310 pages)

The 1969 tactical report on HAVE DOUGHNUT, the 1968 project in which Air Force and Navy pilots secretly flew a Soviet-built MiG-21 at Area 51 against US fighters to learn how to beat it. Israel had lent the jet, which it got when an Iraqi pilot defected with it in 1966.

Released 2000-03-23. [Official source](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/intelligence/2013-10-29/area-51-file-secret-aircraft-soviet-migs) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=area51-have-doughnut)

**Skylab Photographs Area 51** (Document, CIA, 1974, 5 pages)

A 1974 memo to CIA Director William Colby reporting that Skylab astronauts had photographed the one place they had been told not to (the name is blacked out; it was Area 51), and that NASA wanted the picture released while Defense Department agencies wanted it withheld. Colby's handwritten note questions the need to protect it, since the Soviets had it from their own satellites, and asks what it really reveals.

Released 2004-10-12. [Official source](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp80m01048a000800210008-5) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=area51-skylab-memo)

**CIA Map of Area 51** (Photo, CIA, 1992)

A CIA map from the 1992 U-2 and OXCART history that marks Area 51 and Groom Lake beside the Atomic Energy Commission's Nevada Test Site. It was released in 2013 with the less-redacted history, a release widely reported as the US government's first acknowledgement of the base.

Released 2013-06-25. [Official source](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/0000190094) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=area51-groom-lake-map)

**The U-2 and OXCART Programs** (Document, CIA, 1992, 54 pages)

Chapter 2 of the CIA's 1992 internal history of its U-2 and OXCART spy-plane programs, from the less-redacted version released in 2013 that names Area 51. It tells how the CIA and Lockheed chose Groom Lake in 1955 and says U-2 and OXCART flights accounted for more than half of all UFO reports in the late 1950s and most of the 1960s.

Released 2013-06-25. [Official source](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/0000190094) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=area51-u2-oxcart-history)

**The SR-71** (Film, US Air Force, 1997, 1:20)

A short U.S. Air Force film of the SR-71 Blackbird, the Air Force's two-seat reconnaissance version of the CIA's A-12, with air-to-air shots, cockpit instruments at about Mach 3 above 80,000 feet, a takeoff and a landing. President Johnson revealed the SR-71 in July 1964; the CIA history says he was meant to say 'RS-71'.

## Psychic spies

**Experiments: Uri Geller at SRI** (Document, CIA, 1973, 31 pages)

Stanford Research Institute notes, from the CIA's files on its SRI contract, on eight days of tests in August 1973 in which Uri Geller tried to copy drawings he could not see, either locked in a shielded room or while the targets were drawn on the East Coast. The researchers wrote that he had shown paranormal perception 'in a convincing and unambiguous manner'.

Released 2003-03-28. [Official source](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp79-00999a000300030027-0) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=psychic-uri-geller-sri)

**Pat Price's Crane Drawing** (Photo, CIA, 1974)

A page from Kenneth Kress's 1977 CIA article comparing a crane at a Soviet research site near Semipalatinsk (URDF-3) with the crane that remote viewer Pat Price drew at SRI in July 1974, after being given the site's coordinates and told it was a Soviet test site. Analysts agreed the crane was accurate, but most of Price's other data were wrong or could not be checked.

Released 2003-04-18. [Official source](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp96-00791r000200030040-0) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=psychic-price-crane-drawing)

**Parapsychology in Intelligence** (Document, CIA, 1977, 12 pages)

CIA officer Kenneth Kress's account of the agency's psychic research, written for its classified journal Studies in Intelligence in 1977. It covers the SRI work, Pat Price's viewing of a Soviet test site and Price naming Kress's CIA job, and argues the results were never properly assessed.

Released 2003-04-18. [Official source](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp96-00791r000200030040-0) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=psychic-kress-review)

**Gateway Process Assessment** (Document, US Army, 1983, 29 pages)

A 1983 report by Army Lt. Col. Wayne McDonnell assessing the Monroe Institute's Gateway Experience, a course of audio tapes meant to synchronize the brain's hemispheres (Hemi-Sync). It tries to explain the course's claims of out-of-body states and perception beyond time and space in terms of physics, drawing on Itzhak Bentov's models.

Released 2003-09-10. [Official source](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp96-00788r001700210016-5) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=psychic-gateway-process)

**Psychics and the Dozier Kidnapping** (Document, US Army, 1983, 10 pages)

Slides from a 1983 Army briefing on its remote viewers' sessions to locate Brigadier General James Dozier, kidnapped by the Red Brigades in Verona in December 1981. The slide for 20 January 1982 reads 'The name is Padua'; Italian police, helped by a US Army intelligence unit, freed Dozier from a Padua apartment on 28 January 1982.

Released 2000-08-08. [Official source](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp96-00788r001700370004-1) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=psychic-dozier-padua)

**Mars Exploration, May 22, 1984** (Document, US Army, 1984, 9 pages)

Transcript of a May 1984 remote-viewing session in which the viewer held a sealed envelope, opened only afterwards, with a card reading 'The planet Mars. Released 2000-08-08. [Official source](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp96-00788r001900760001-9) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=psychic-mars-1984)

**Coordinate Remote Viewing (1985)** (Document, US Army, 1985, 40 pages)

A secret February 1985 working paper on Coordinate Remote Viewing, the training method developed by Ingo Swann with SRI, in which a viewer tries to describe a site given only its geographic coordinates. It sets out the six training stages and includes a sample session.

Released 2000-08-07. [Official source](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp96-00788r001000400001-7) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=psychic-crv-manual)

**An Evaluation of Remote Viewing** (Document, CIA, 1995, 206 pages)

The 1995 review of the government's remote-viewing program by the American Institutes for Research, commissioned by the CIA, with assessments by statistician Jessica Utts and psychologist Ray Hyman. It found remote viewing had never been used to guide intelligence operations and concluded that continued use was not warranted; the program was closed that year.

## Mind control

**Project Bluebird** (Document, CIA, 1950, 29 pages)

April 1950 memo asking the Director of Central Intelligence to approve Project Bluebird, interrogation teams that would use the polygraph, drugs and hypnotism, sent to him directly rather than through the Projects Review Committee because of its sensitivity. The file also holds 1952 memos on its successor, Project ARTICHOKE.

Released 2004-01-29. [Official source](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp83-01042r000800010003-1) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=mind-project-bluebird)

**MKULTRA Subproject 35** (Document, CIA, 1954, 71 pages)

CIA file on the MKULTRA subproject that secretly put $375,000 through a front foundation toward a new wing of Georgetown University Hospital so the agency could use part of it for its chemical and biological research. It includes a May 5, 1955 draft listing what the CIA's Chemical Division was trying to develop, from substances that 'promote illogical thinking' to methods that cause amnesia.

Released 1977. [Official source](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/00017432) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=mind-subproject-35)

**Subproject 42 (Midnight Climax)** (Document, CIA, 1956, 1 pages)

One-page memo in which Sidney Gottlieb, who ran MKULTRA, explains that people who covertly gave drugs to unwitting subjects for a CIA safehouse run by narcotics agent George White would be paid in cash, without receipts. Subproject 42 funded White's San Francisco safehouse, the operation known as Midnight Climax.

Released 1977. [Official source](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/00748553) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=mind-midnight-climax)

**Effects of LSD on Troops Marching** (Film, US Army, 1958, 5:29)

U.S. Army Chemical Warfare Laboratories film in which volunteer soldiers try to do close-order drill after being given LSD, and the drill falls apart. The Army was testing LSD as a battlefield incapacitant in the same years as the CIA's drug experiments.

Released 1963-05-29. [Official source](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/2123707) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=mind-lsd-troops-film)

**MKULTRA Subproject 119** (Document, CIA, 1959, 48 pages)

CIA file on an MKULTRA subproject that paid for a survey of research on recording bioelectric signals from the human body and 'activation of human behavior by remote means'. Its 1960 project memo lists remote electronic activation of the human organism as one of five study areas.

Released 1998-11-16. [Official source](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/00017376) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=mind-subproject-119)

**Report of Inspection of MKULTRA** (Document, CIA, 1963, 46 pages)

The CIA Inspector General's report on MKULTRA, prepared in a single copy, which found that the agency had been covertly testing drugs and other materials on unwitting US citizens since 1955 and recommended ending that testing in the United States. Released 2019-04-03. [Official source](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/06767515) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=mind-ig-report-1963)

## Nuclear

**Trinity Fireball at 16 ms** (Photo, Manhattan Project, 1945)

Berlyn Brixner's photograph of the Trinity fireball 0.016 seconds after the first nuclear explosion, in New Mexico on 16 July 1945. Fireball photos with elapsed times were declassified in July 1947, and physicist G. I. Taylor later used them to estimate the bomb's yield.

Released 1947-07-17. [Official source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Trinity_Test_Fireball_16ms.jpg) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=nuclear-trinity-fireball)

**Operation Ivy** (Film, US Air Force, 1952, 9:49)

Silent 10-minute National Archives reel of Operation Ivy, the Air Force film of Mike, the first full-scale hydrogen bomb test, with actor Reed Hadley as host. Mike yielded 10.4 megatons on 1 November 1952 and left a crater where the island of Elugelab had been; a censored version of the film was released to the public in 1954.

Released 1954. [Official source](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/66458) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=nuclear-operation-ivy-film)

**Castle Bravo** (Photo, US Department of Defense, 1954)

Aerial photograph of the Castle Bravo fireball over Bikini Atoll on 1 March 1954, the largest nuclear test the United States ever conducted. Bravo yielded 15 megatons, nearly three times what its planners expected, and its fallout sickened Marshall Islanders and contaminated 23 Japanese fishermen.

Released 1993. [Official source](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/146763394) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=nuclear-castle-bravo)

**SAC Atomic Weapons Requirements Study** (Document, Strategic Air Command, 1956, 21 pages)

Title page, contents and introduction of Strategic Air Command's June 1956 study of the atomic weapons it would need for a war in 1959. The full study listed more than 1,100 Soviet bloc airfields and more than 1,200 cities as targets, with 'population' as a target in cities such as Moscow, Leningrad, East Berlin, Warsaw and Beijing.

Released 2014-10. [Official source](https://nsarchive2.gwu.edu/nukevault/ebb538-Cold-War-Nuclear-Target-List-Declassified-First-Ever/) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=nuclear-sac-target-study)

**Hardtack I: Nutmeg test film** (Film, LLNL, 1958, 0:59)

Color film of Nutmeg, a 25-kiloton Livermore test device fired on a barge at Bikini Atoll on 22 May 1958 during Operation Hardtack I. Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory declassified the film and posted it online in March 2017, in a project to save decaying nuclear test films.

Released 2017-03. [Official source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XnrLY-phipw) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=nuclear-hardtack-nutmeg-film)

**Project A119: Lunar Research Flights** (Document, US Air Force, 1959, 190 pages)

Volume I of a 1959 study by Leonard Reiffel of the Armour Research Foundation for the Air Force Special Weapons Center on what a nuclear explosion near the Moon could reveal; Carl Sagan is listed as a contributor. The plan, later known as Project A119, was never carried out and became publicly known when Reiffel described it in 2000.

Released 2000. [Official source](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/GOVPUB-D301-PURL-gpo32511) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=nuclear-project-a119)

**Starfish Prime** (Photo, US Department of Defense, 1962)

Photograph of the Starfish Prime burst and the aurora it made, taken from atop Haleakala on Maui. The 1.4-megaton warhead exploded about 400 km above the Pacific near Johnston Atoll on 9 July 1962, and its electromagnetic pulse knocked out about 300 streetlights in Hawaii.

Released 1993. [Official source](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/146764182) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=nuclear-starfish-prime)

**Goldsboro Revisited** (Document, Sandia Laboratories, 1969, 2 pages)

A two-page 1969 memo by Sandia weapons safety supervisor Parker F. Jones about the January 1961 B-52 breakup over North Carolina, which dropped two Mark 39 hydrogen bombs near Goldsboro. Jones concluded that the bomb lacked adequate safety for the B-52 airborne alert role and that one low-voltage switch stood between the United States and a major catastrophe.

Released 2013-09-20. [Official source](https://prologue.blogs.archives.gov/2021/01/22/brush-with-catastrophe-the-day-the-u-s-almost-nuked-itself/) · [Open in the explorer](https://abstracts.co/a/declassified?a_file=nuclear-goldsboro-revisited)

**The Situation Room** (Photo, White House, 2011)

Pete Souza's photograph of President Obama, Vice President Biden and the national security team in the White House Situation Room during Operation Neptune Spear, the raid on Osama bin Laden's compound in Pakistan. The White House released it the next day with a note that a classified document in the picture had been obscured.
